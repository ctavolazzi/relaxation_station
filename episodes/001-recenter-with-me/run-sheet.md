# 001 Recenter with me

- Moment: morning or midday (the sample opens with "recenter your energy today")
- Turn: The Meeting (the host's "simulated meeting")
- Cup: coffee, with tea offered. A second cup in frame is a nice touch.
- Place: anywhere quiet
- Plan: about ten minutes, one take. The times below are a plan. Real timestamps come from the recording.
- If you blank: sip.

Lines in quotes are the host's own words from the pitch (`docs/origin.md`), in order. Everything else is
direction. Seeds marked "mine" are the session's suggestions: rewrite them or ignore them.

Before you press record: lens at eye level, window to one side, room quiet, this card beside the lens
(`docs/production.md`).

## 1. Arrive (plan 0:00)
A breath of silence on the lens. Smile.
> "Hello, welcome, it's good to see you again."

## 2. Offer (0:30)
Show the cup.
> "Here's some coffee. If you want it, I also have tea. But I am the Coffee Jesus, so I'll be drinking a coffee with you today."

## 3. Invite (1:10)
> "I'm here to invite you to recalibrate and recenter your energy today with me. Would you like to do that?"

Wait long enough that someone could answer.
> "I'll give it a try. What does it mean?"

(These two lines read like the viewer's answer, said in your voice. Keep them if they feel right.)
> "Well, it means that you and I are going to be sharing space with each other for the rest of this video, if you're open to that."

## 4. Share (2:20)
> "What I'd like to do is connect with you, simply share this coffee, or if you chose the tea, share the tea,"

Sip. A little laugh.
> "and explore this moment together first."

## 5. Breathe (3:10)
> "Let's take a breath."

In slowly, out slowly, on the lens. Count to five before you speak again.
> "And then one more."

## 6. Check in (4:10)
Pause three to five seconds after each question. The silence is the content.
> "How do you feel? Are you relaxed? Are you tense? Are you laying down with your eyes closed?"

The silly one goes last. Smile and let it land.
> "Did you spill any of that coffee on you when you drank it?"

## 7. Drift (5:20)
Slow down more. Look up and a little away.
> "Imagine that you didn't have a body at all. Maybe you're floating through the cosmos. Maybe there's not much difference between floating through the cosmos and being a human on a planet."

Pause about ten seconds.
> "Take a moment to just zoom out, let your imagination wander."

Pause for a long time. Twenty seconds is fine. Come back to the lens when you are ready to land.

## 8. Sip (7:00)
Sip slowly.
> "That's good coffee."

## 9. Turn: The Meeting (7:20)
> "Today, we have a few things we need to talk about."

Then it becomes a simulated meeting. You are at the head of a very small, very calm table, and the viewer
is the only one in the room. Seeds (mine):

- Item one, how we're doing. "We checked in earlier. We're doing okay. Moving on."
- Item two, the thing you walked in carrying. "We don't have to solve it. We only have to look at it for a second, and then we can set it down by the door."
- Item three, one small kind thing for the rest of today. Let them pick it silently. Wait.
- Any other business? Wait four seconds. "No? Great."
- "Meeting adjourned. Take your coffee with you."

## 10. Close (9:30)
Seed (mine):
> "Thank you for sharing this with me. You can go back to your day now, or stay a little longer. Either way, you're welcome here."

Stay on the lens for one breath after the last word. Then stop.
