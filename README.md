# Relaxation Station

Calm, simple videos on **The Coffee Jesus** channel. One camera, one voice, eye contact, and a slow, kind
pace, like a good friend keeping you company while you decompress or start your day.

Empathy, no more and no less. No new persona. No big production. Keep it simple, silly.

This repo is the home base: the format, the episode run sheets, the packaging rules, and the record of
what we decided and why. It is also a stepping stone. The end game is the host's to share, so everything
here is built in small pieces that carry forward whatever it turns out to be.

## The show in one screen

- **What it is:** the host talks to the camera, slowly and kindly, and shares a coffee (or tea) with you.
  A little breathing, a little imagination, a little humor. Then a gentle middle (a pretend meeting, a
  real listen, or a few wishes for your day) and a warm goodbye.
- **What it is not:** ASMR, a challenge, a lecture, advice, a pitch, therapy, or a big production.
- **Why it is easy:** the face, the voice and the channel already exist. A phone, a window and a cup are
  enough, so an episode can happen any time of day, from anywhere, including on the road.

## Make an episode

1. Copy `episodes/_template/` to `episodes/NNN-slug/`.
2. Fill in the run sheet. It is a beat sheet, not a script. Keep it beside the lens.
3. Record top to bottom in one take. A flub is fine: laugh, sip, keep going.
4. Trim the head and tail, set the loudness, and listen to the first minute on headphones.
5. **Make the thumbnail.** Every video ships with one, checked at 320 px wide.
6. Title, description, playlist "Relaxation Station", upload.

Details are in `docs/production.md` and `docs/packaging.md`.
