# The format

The host sits with you, looks at you, and keeps you company for about ten minutes. Slow, warm, a little
funny. The viewer does nothing.

Rules marked **user** are the host's words (`origin.md`). Rules marked **assumed** are a first guess and
the host's to change.

## The promise to the viewer

What a viewer can count on, every time. If an episode breaks one of these, fix the episode.

1. **It is you and me.** The host looks at the lens and talks to one person. (user)
2. **Slow and kind.** Unhurried, warm, a little funny. A normal soft voice. Not ASMR. (user)
3. **Company, not coaching.** Empathy and nothing else: no advice, no fixing, no pitch. (user: "no more, no less")
4. **No pressure.** Nothing to finish and nothing to do right. Pause, leave, come back, or fall asleep. (assumed)
5. **A familiar door.** Every episode opens the same way: a greeting, a cup, an invitation. (assumed)
6. **Company, not care.** If someone is really struggling, the description says so and points to real help. (assumed)

## The shape of an episode: the Pour

Ten beats, in the same order every time. What the host says inside each beat is ad-libbed. The times are a
plan for a ten-minute episode, not a measurement.

| # | Beat | What happens | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive | A beat of silence on the lens, a smile, "Hello, welcome." | 0:00 |
| 2 | Offer | The cup: coffee, or tea if you like. The small joke about being the Coffee Jesus. | 0:30 |
| 3 | Invite | Ask to recenter together and wait for the answer. Say what that means: sharing space. | 1:10 |
| 4 | Share | Share the cup. A little laugh. "Explore this moment together." | 2:20 |
| 5 | Breathe | One breath. Then one more. | 3:10 |
| 6 | Check in | Three or four gentle questions with real pauses. The last one is silly. | 4:10 |
| 7 | Drift | "Zoom out." Slow imagining, with long silences. | 5:20 |
| 8 | Sip | "That's good coffee." The landing beat. | 7:00 |
| 9 | Turn | The middle of the episode. Different every time (below). | 7:20 |
| 10 | Close | Thank you, a release, a welcome back. | 9:30 |

## The Turn: pick one per episode

The Turn is where each episode becomes its own. Start with these three. More are parked in `ideas.md`.

- **The Meeting.** A very small, very calm pretend meeting about the viewer's day. Straight face, soft
  humor, a gentle agenda, "meeting adjourned." Good for after work and rough days.
  (user: "a simulated meeting")
- **The Listen.** One open question ("What was today like?"), then really listen: stay on the lens, nod, an
  occasional "mm" or "that makes sense," and long silences. No advice. Good for after work and evening.
  (from the host's "listening to you decompress"; the shape is assumed)
- **The Wishes.** A few plain wishes for the viewer's day or night. Good for morning and goodnight.
  (from the host's "wishing you a nice, positive, beautiful morning"; the shape is assumed)

## Moments

The same Pour fits any time of day. Say the moment in the title and let it color the Offer and the Turn.

| Moment | The feeling | Likely Turn |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | a gentle, positive start | The Wishes |
| Midday | a reset between things | The Meeting |
| After work | put it down and decompress | The Listen or The Meeting |
| Evening | wind down, permission to stop | The Listen |
| Anywhere | the host is on the road, and the place is part of the welcome | any |

## Delivery

- **Pace.** Well under conversation speed. Count three after a question and five after a breath.
- **Silence is the content.** After a question, wait for the answer you cannot hear. The first silence will
  feel too long. That is about right.
- **Eyes.** On the lens for every ask and every kindness. During the Drift you may look up and a little away,
  then come back to the lens to land it.
- **Voice.** A normal soft speaking voice, not a whisper. Smile with your eyes. A little laugh is part of the show.
- **The cup.** A prop and a reset. If you blank, sip. A sip is always allowed.
- **Humor.** Gentle and self-aware, never at the viewer's expense ("Did you spill any of that coffee on you?").
- **Ad-lib inside the shape.** The run sheet says where you are, not what you must say. The anchor lines are
  there for when you want them.

## Words

Reach for: "Welcome." "Come sit." "You don't have to do anything." "Of course." "That sounds like a lot."
"Take your time." "No rush." "I'm here."

Leave out: "you should," "you need to," "just relax," "calm down," "don't worry," "everything happens for a
reason," advice of any kind, claims that anything cures or heals, and any pitch. Telling someone to relax or
not to worry tends to land as dismissal. Invite instead: "let's see if the shoulders want to come down."

## Length

About ten minutes (assumed, D6). Long enough to settle, short enough to record in one take and watch on a
break. Revisit after three episodes, using average view duration from YouTube Studio.

## What it is not

ASMR. A challenge (that is the RDA series). Meditation instruction or therapy. A livestream. A big production.
