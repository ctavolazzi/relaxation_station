## Open questions the first episodes can answer

Whatever the end game turns out to be, these answers will help. Numbers come from YouTube Studio only,
written down with the date they were read.

- Which moment (morning, after work, evening) is watched the longest?
- Does the Turn hold attention, and which Turn does best?
- Do people come back, and how many?
- What do the comments say? Do viewers say they felt heard?
- Is ten minutes right, or do viewers want shorter or longer?
